Sample case data marked **Mock data** is about a fictional brand and was written to demonstrate the product.

## Analysis and opinion, not statements of fact

A report is our analysis of how the public reacted to a campaign. The diagnosis is the explanation that the collected evidence supports best. It is an informed opinion, not a statement of fact about the brand, its people, its internal decisions or its intentions, and it is not a finding that anyone acted wrongly.

Confidence scores and the Backlash score describe the strength and tone of the public evidence we collected. They are not probabilities of a business outcome and say nothing about sales, profit or media performance.

## Limits of the evidence

- **Public signals only.** We do not use sales, performance or ad-account data, so a campaign can perform well commercially and still draw a negative public reaction, or the other way round.
- **A sample, not everyone.** Each report is based on a limited sample of reactions — typically 30 to 80 items. The people who comment online are not a representative sample of all customers.
- **A point in time.** Evidence is collected on the date shown on the report. Reactions, coverage and the campaign itself may have changed since.
- **Sources can change.** Posts and articles may be edited or deleted after we collect them, so a source link may no longer show what we quoted.

## Use of AI

Campaign Forensics uses AI throughout. The investigation is run by an AI agent (Manus), public content is collected with automated tools (Apify), and concept images, video, voice-over and music are generated by AI models (via fal.ai). People define the method and review the output, but AI can misread context, sarcasm or language, and can make mistakes. That is why every claim in a report links to the evidence behind it: check the sources before you rely on a conclusion.

## Generated creative

Logo, identity, packaging, key-visual and video concepts in a report are generated from the diagnosis to illustrate a possible fix. Each one is labelled as a concept. They are not official designs of any brand, must not be presented as such, and may resemble existing marks or works. Before any commercial use, check trademark, copyright and other rights yourself; that responsibility is yours.
