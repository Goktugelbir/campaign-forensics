## Who is responsible for your data

The controller is the Campaign Forensics team, an independent project built at the 10X Your Brand Hackathon in Istanbul. Until a company is incorporated, the team acts as controller. You can reach us at the address at the bottom of this page. We have not appointed a data protection officer; we are not required to at our current size. If we offer the service to people in the EU on a regular basis, we will appoint an EU representative under Article 27 GDPR and name them here.

## What personal data we process and where it comes from

**Data you give us (Article 13).** When you join the waitlist, start a case, contact us or send a data request, we receive your e-mail address and, if you provide them, your name, your message and the details of the campaign you want investigated.

**Data we collect from public sources (Article 14).** To investigate a campaign we collect publicly available reactions: news articles, comments on social media and video platforms, and forum posts. Before anything is stored we remove usernames, profile links and profile photos. What remains is the text of the comment, its date, public engagement counts (for example likes) and a link to the post. We do not build profiles of individuals or combine data about a person across sources.

We do not intend to process special categories of data (Article 9). If a public comment happens to reveal such information, we rely on Article 9(2)(e) — data manifestly made public by the person — and remove it on request.

## Why we process it and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Sending the report you asked for, answering your message | Performance of a contract or steps at your request — Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Telling waitlist members when access opens | Legitimate interest in responding to your sign-up — Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Product-update e-mails | Your consent, given with a separate unticked box — Art. 6(1)(a) |
| Analysing public reactions to a campaign | Legitimate interest in independent analysis and commentary — Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Handling data requests and legal claims | Legal obligation and legitimate interest — Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) |

## Our legitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights. The analysis concerns how the public reacted to a brand's campaign, not the people who reacted. We only use content that its authors published openly, we keep the minimum needed (the text, not the identity), and every item can be removed on request. We consider that comment authors can reasonably expect public posts about a public campaign to be quoted in commentary on that campaign.

## Who receives your data

We use a small number of processors, bound by data processing agreements under Article 28 GDPR and acting only on our instructions:

- **Apify** — collecting public content.
- **Manus** — running the investigation workflow.
- **fal.ai** — generating concept images and video from the creative brief. No personal data is sent to it.
- Our e-mail provider, for messages you send us.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

## Transfers outside the EU / EEA

Some processors store or process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where the destination country has no adequacy decision from the European Commission, transfers are protected by the Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Article 46(2)(c)) or, for US recipients certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, by that adequacy decision. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

## How long we keep it

- Waitlist e-mail addresses: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
- Case requests and correspondence: up to 12 months after the report is delivered.
- Raw collected content: 90 days after the report is delivered. Anonymised evidence in a published report stays with that report until someone asks for its removal.
- Data requests: as long as needed to show we handled them, at most 3 years.

## Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 21 GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- have inaccurate data **corrected**;
- have your data **erased**, including having a piece of evidence removed from a report;
- **restrict** how we use it while a question is being resolved;
- receive data you gave us in a portable format (**data portability**);
- **object** at any time to processing based on legitimate interests — including the use of your public comment as evidence. We will then stop unless we have compelling grounds that override your interests;
- **withdraw consent** to product-update e-mails at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Use the [Data requests](/legal/data-request) page. We answer within one month; for complex requests we may extend this by two further months and will tell you why.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or where you believe the infringement took place.

## If your comment appears in a report

We cannot contact comment authors individually: we deliberately do not store usernames or profile links, and identifying people only to notify them would mean collecting more data about them. Under Article 14(5)(b) we therefore make this notice public instead. If you recognise your comment in a report, use the "Report this evidence" link on the card or the [Data requests](/legal/data-request) page with the evidence ID (for example ev_014) and we will remove it.

## Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing (Article 22). The diagnosis is about a campaign, not about the people who commented on it.

## Do you have to give us your data?

No. You only need to give us an e-mail address if you want to receive a report, join the waitlist or get a reply. Without it we cannot send you anything.

We will update this page when our processing changes and show the new date at the top.
