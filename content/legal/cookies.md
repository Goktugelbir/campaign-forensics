## What we use

The site sets no cookies of its own. The framework may keep strictly necessary technical state in your browser while you navigate (for example, the report tab you are on is kept in the page address, not in a cookie).

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| — | Campaign Forensics | No cookies are set | — | — |

## What we don't use

No analytics cookies, no advertising or marketing cookies, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting.

## If this changes

If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask for consent first with a banner where **Accept** and **Reject** are equally prominent, nothing is pre-ticked, and **Preferences** lets you choose by category. This page and its table will be updated before that happens.

## Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will keep working.
