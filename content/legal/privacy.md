## Who we are

Campaign Forensics is a campaign-diagnosis service built by an independent team at the 10X Your Brand Hackathon, Istanbul. Until a company is incorporated, the team acts as data controller. Contact us at the address at the bottom of this page.

## What data we collect

**(a) Visitors and form users.** When you join the waitlist, open a case or contact us, we receive your e-mail address and, if you give it, your name and message. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

**(b) Public content we analyze.** To investigate a campaign we collect publicly available reactions — news articles, video and social-media comments, forum posts. Before anything is stored, usernames, profile links and profile photos are removed. Comment text is kept anonymously and always linked to the original post, never to a profile.

## Why we use it

- To send you the report you requested or reply to your message (performance of a contract / your request).
- To let you know when we open access, only if you joined the waitlist (legitimate interest), and to send product updates only if you ticked the separate opt-in box (consent).
- To investigate campaigns using public signals (legitimate interest in independent analysis and commentary).

## Who processes data with us

We use a small number of processors: our hosting provider, **Apify** (collecting public content), **Manus** (running the investigation) and **fal.ai** (generating concept images and video from the creative brief — no personal data is sent to it). Each processes data only on our instructions.

## International transfers

Some of these services process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission or on its Standard Contractual Clauses (Articles 45–46 GDPR). Details are in the [GDPR Notice](/legal/gdpr).

## How long we keep it

- Waitlist e-mails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
- Case requests and correspondence: up to 12 months after the report is delivered.
- Raw collected content: 90 days after the report is delivered; the anonymised evidence in a published report stays with the report.

## Security

Access to raw data is limited to the people running the investigation. API keys are kept in environment variables, never in the code or the website.

## Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, or have a piece of evidence removed from a report. Use the [Data requests](/legal/data-request) page. We answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority in the EU — see the [GDPR Notice](/legal/gdpr).

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top.
