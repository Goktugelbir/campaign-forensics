## Who can use this page

- People whose public comment appears as evidence in a report.
- Brands we have analyzed.
- Anyone on our waitlist or who has contacted us.

## What you can ask for

- **Information** — what data we hold about you and how we use it.
- **Correction** — fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
- **Deletion** — delete your data.
- **Remove evidence** — take a piece of evidence out of a report.
- **Objection** — object to how we process your data.

## What to include

The report link or the evidence ID (for example ev_014), your e-mail address, and a short description. We may ask for something that shows the comment is yours; we will never ask for a password.

## Response time

We respond within one month, as required by Article 12(3) GDPR. For complex requests we may extend this by two further months and will tell you why within the first month.
