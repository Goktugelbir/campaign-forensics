## Public content only

We only analyze content that is publicly visible without logging in. Private accounts, closed groups and anything behind a login are never collected.

## No usernames, no profiles

Usernames, display names, profile links and profile photos are removed before evidence is stored, and never shown in a report. Each piece of evidence links to the post or article it came from — not to the person who wrote it. Comments are kept as short, anonymous text (up to about 500 characters).

## Out of scope

We do not analyze political, religious, ethnic or other sensitive content, and we do not produce creative on those themes.

## Balance

A diagnosis is only credible if it looks at all the evidence. Supportive and neutral reactions are collected and shown alongside negative ones, and hypotheses that the evidence doesn't support are shown as rejected rather than hidden.

## Tools and platform terms

Evidence is collected with Apify actors and processed in a Manus task. We keep request volumes small (typically 30–80 items per case) and follow the terms of the tools and platforms we use.

## Removal

If your comment appears in a report and you want it removed, or you represent a brand we analyzed, use the [Data requests](/legal/data-request) page and include the evidence ID (for example ev_014). We respond within 30 days at the latest.
