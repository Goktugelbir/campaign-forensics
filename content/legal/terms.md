## The service

Campaign Forensics is a service that analyzes public reactions to a marketing campaign, tests six explanations against the evidence, and produces a diagnosis, a prescription and a creative concept. Features and availability may change over time.

## Your obligations

- Only request analyses you are authorized to request, for example for your own brand, your client, a prospect you are pitching, or for independent research.
- Do not use a report in a misleading or defamatory way, or present it as the view of the analyzed brand.
- Do not try to use the service to identify, profile or contact the people whose public comments appear as evidence.

## What a report is

A report is an interpretation of public signals — the explanation the available evidence supports best. It is not a certainty, not an audit of performance data, and not a guarantee of results.

## Intellectual property

You may use your report and the generated concept for your own business purposes. Brand names, logos and campaign materials of analyzed brands remain the property of their owners. Quoted evidence belongs to its authors and is shown in short, anonymised form for analysis and commentary.

## AI-generated content

Generated images and videos are concepts derived from the diagnosis. They are not official designs of any brand. Checking trademark, copyright and likeness rights before any commercial use is your responsibility.
